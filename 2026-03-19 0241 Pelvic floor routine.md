## Morning Micro-Routine — 5 min
*Do before checking your phone. Non-negotiable.*

---

### 1. Isometric Pelvic Rotation Reset — 1 min
**Targets:** Directly corrects right anterior pelvic rotation using muscle energy technique

- Lie on your back, knees bent
- Place your **right foot on top of your left knee** at roughly a 30° bend
- Press your **right foot DOWN** into your left knee
- Simultaneously push your **left knee UP** against your right foot
- These two forces cancel each other out — **no movement should happen**
- Hold **6 seconds**, rest 5 seconds
- Repeat **5 times**

> ✅ This is a clinical muscle energy technique specifically for right anterior pelvic rotation
> ✅ No core tensing required — the opposing leg forces do all the work
> ✅ You should feel the right hip and glute area mildly engage during the hold
> ❌ If movement is happening, increase the opposing force from both sides equally
> 💡 Search "Hands On rotated pelvis correction" on YouTube to see this performed visually

---

### 2. Crocodile Breathing — 2 min
**Targets:** Pelvic floor tone reset, diaphragm activation, sets nervous system baseline for the day

- Lie face down, forehead on back of hands, legs slightly apart and fully relaxed
- Inhale slowly through nose — belly pushes into floor, lower back gently rises
- Exhale fully through mouth — everything drops and softens completely
- On every inhale, consciously let pelvic floor **expand and drop downward**
- **4 sec in / 6 sec out — 12 breaths total**

> ✅ Lower back rises and falls visibly — chest stays mostly still
> ❌ Only chest moving — place hands on lower back and breathe into them instead
> ✅ Most important exercise in the entire routine — never skip it

---

### 3. Supine Reverse Kegel — 1 min
**Targets:** Directly downtrains pelvic floor hypertonia, trains sphincter release reflex

- Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat, arms relaxed at sides
- Inhale slowly — let pelvic floor **drop, spread, and bulge downward**
- Think: sit bones spreading apart, perineum softening toward the floor
- Exhale — floor returns naturally, no squeezing whatsoever
- **8 slow reps**

> ✅ Most important exercise for bladder emptying and pelvic tension
> ✅ Active version of what your body does when you tuck tailbone and unlock knees
> ❌ Never squeeze or lift — if you feel a kegel happening, reset and slow down
> ❌ Never hold your breath during this

---

### 4. 90/90 Right Hip Stretch — 90 sec
**Targets:** Right anterior pelvic rotation — structural root cause
*Do this on the floor — the only exercise requiring you to get up*

- Sit on floor, right leg forward at 90°, left leg behind at 90° (Z-shape)
- Sit tall — do not collapse or round forward
- Lean gently forward over right shin until deep stretch felt in right glute/outer hip
- Hold entire 90 seconds, breathe slowly — let it deepen on each exhale
- **Right side only** — intentionally asymmetric

> ✅ Feel it deep in right glute or outer hip
> ❌ Feeling it in your knee — shuffle foot further from your body
> 💡 Do this right after the isometric reset while the correction is fresh — it stretches what the reset just repositioned

## Before Bed Routine — 10 min
*Main session. Do on floor or firm surface.*

---

### 5. Supine Figure-4 — Left Side Focus — 2 min
**Targets:** Left SI joint decompression and restriction

- Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat
- Cross **left** ankle over right knee
- Draw right thigh slowly toward chest until deep stretch felt in left outer hip
- **90 seconds left side, 30 seconds right side**
- Breathe into the stretch — feel left hip release on each exhale

> ✅ Feel it in left outer hip and glute — not the knee
> ✅ Directly decompresses the left SI joint — compressed counterpart to your right rotation
> 💡 Left side becoming progressively easier to get into = rotation is resolving

---

### 6. Neural Flossing — Sciatic — 2 min
**Targets:** Sciatic nerve sensitization, false hamstring tightness, both legs

- Sit upright on edge of bed or chair, feet flat on floor
- Straighten right leg, flex foot toward you — hold 2 sec
- Simultaneously point foot down + drop chin to chest — hold 2 sec
- Alternate slowly — **10 reps right, 10 reps left**
- Smooth and controlled throughout — this is a glide, not a stretch

> ✅ Mild tingling or dull pulling sensation is normal and expected
> ❌ Sharp or lingering sensation after stopping — reduce range immediately
> ❌ Never bounce or force the movement
> 💡 Sensation gradually reducing over weeks = nerve tension releasing

---

### 7. Left Glute Clamshell — 3 min
**Targets:** Left glute activation, pelvic stability asymmetry

- Lie on right side, hips stacked, knees bent at 90°, feet together
- Place hand on left hip to monitor — pelvis must not roll back at all
- Lift top (left) knee as high as possible without pelvis moving
- Lower slowly — **3 full seconds on the way down**
- **3 sets of 10 reps**, 20 sec rest between sets
- Then flip: **1 set of 8 reps** right side (maintenance only)

> ✅ Burn should be felt deep in left glute specifically
> ❌ Feeling it in outer thigh or lower back — reduce range and slow down
> 💡 Week 3+: loop a belt or towel above knees for added resistance once this feels easy

---

### 8. Perineal Breathing — 2 min
**Targets:** Pelvic floor release coordination, sphincter re-tightening, perineal stiffness

- Lie on back, knees bent, one hand on lower belly
- Inhale slowly — belly rises AND perineum softens and drops simultaneously
- Exhale — gentle soft inward lift of lower belly (not a crunch — subtle)
- **10 slow breath cycles**

> ✅ Think of breathing directly into the perineum on each inhale
> ✅ Do 3 of these breaths immediately before urinating — primes the release reflex
> ✅ Directly retrains the sphincter to stay open for a complete void
> 💡 Same mechanism as your tailbone tuck discovery — trained actively here

---

### 9. Supine Pelvic Rotation Release — 1 min
**Targets:** Reinforces rotation correction, ends the day in neutral, tracks progress

- Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat
- Slowly rock knees to the right — hold 5 sec
- Return to center
- Slowly rock knees to the left — hold 5 sec
- **4 slow cycles each direction**
- Finish center — 3 belly breaths, let pelvis feel heavy and neutral

> ✅ Right direction will feel freer initially — normal given your rotation
> ✅ Both sides gradually equalizing over weeks = main progress marker
> 💡 Note which side feels more restricted each night — tracks how rotation is resolving

## Urination Protocol — Every Single Void
*Retrains sphincter coordination directly. Takes 10 extra seconds.*

1. Urinate normally until flow slows to a trickle
2. One slow belly breath in
3. Long exhale + deliberate reverse kegel — floor drops completely
4. Wait 5 seconds passively — let remaining flow without any pushing
5. Repeat once more if needed

> ✅ Never strain or push — straining tightens the sphincter reflexively
> ✅ Flow resuming briefly confirms sphincter was still partially closed
> ✅ Consistent use retrains sphincter to stay open for a complete void
> ❌ Skip this and sphincter retraining slows significantly

## How to Know You Are Fully Healed

Stop the active routine when **all of the following** are true
consistently for at least **3–4 weeks in a row:**

**Structural:**
- [ ] Right ilium no longer visibly more prominent than left at rest
- [ ] Pelvic rotation exercise (exercise 9) feels symmetric both directions
- [ ] Standing on left leg feels as stable and easy as right
- [ ] Isometric reset (exercise 1) requires noticeably less effort to feel the correction

**Functional:**
- [ ] Bladder emptying fully reliable without positional compensation
- [ ] Flow completes in a single void — double-release breath no longer needed
- [ ] No perineal stiffness at rest or after prolonged sitting
- [ ] Sciatic and hamstring pulling sensation gone during neural flossing

**Baseline tone:**
- [ ] Pelvic floor feels neutral and low-tension at rest throughout the day
- [ ] No pelvic tension after long sitting sessions
- [ ] Crocodile breathing feels easy, deep, and unrestricted

**When 8 or more boxes are checked consistently — active rehab is complete.**

## After Healing — Maintenance
### 5 min · 3x per week · Permanently

1. Isometric pelvic rotation reset — 1 min
2. Crocodile breathing — 2 min
3. 90/90 right hip stretch right side — 90 sec
4. Left glute clamshell — 1 set of 10 reps

> ⚠️ Long sitting days (travel, desk work, 6+ hours) are the main relapse trigger
> After any such day, do the full morning routine the following morning

## Minimum If You Miss Time

If you only have 5 minutes on a given day, do these in order:

1. Crocodile breathing — 2 min
2. Reverse kegel with breath — 2 min
3. Isometric pelvic reset — 1 min

**Consistent minimums beat inconsistent full sessions every time.**

## Missing Days

| Situation | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Miss 1 day | Continue normally — no adjustment needed |
| Miss 2–3 days | Add extra set of isometric reset and crocodile breathing on return day |
| Miss a full week | Restart from day 1 — don't try to catch up aggressively |
| Chronic inconsistency | Do minimum 3 exercises daily only — consistency over completeness |

> ⚠️ The first 4 weeks are most fragile — the nervous system hasn't built a new default yet.
> After week 6 the gains become significantly more sticky.
