Risking something can be dangerous, 
yet with no risk life is a monochrome story.
Smoking every night yet scared to risk the love, the braveness of facing death is nothing in the face of love. 
Scared to loose you yet without risk you'll be fine anyway, and the thought of that puts me on fire. 
But if I take off the coward mask, you'll be the captain of the coward boat.
