O stars please tell me—
Has she moved on, or is she waiting

Has the pain healed—
Or still visible 

Have my wrongdoings been this painful—
Should I commit seppuku

Can I be forgiven—
Or do I live with this pain

My dear I wish to be back to the old times—
Where sweet talks and laughs we shared 

Space is killing—
Can we close it again

The scent is suffocating—
The corpse is numb, heart is heavy
