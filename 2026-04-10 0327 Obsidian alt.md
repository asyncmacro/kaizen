Obsidian is powerful which can be overwhelming and performance bottle neck, even with high performance PC/mobile.

The idea is like following:
Markdown writing tool with with support of live view and markdown files sidebar list.
Each markdown file have what's called metadata or front-matter which may contain text, tags, or linking to other md files in the vault
A hotkey supported system such as (toggle side bar, focus on title or body, move to top of note or down)
