# Lasting Longer — Progressive Training Routine (Updated)
### Solo practice · Begins after pelvic floor routine completion

> **Your baseline:**
> Under 30 sec without lube · Narrow arousal window · Stop-start attempted but too late
> Years of kegel conditioning to ejaculate faster · Hypertonic pelvic floor (resolved before starting this)

> **Core problems to solve, in order:**
> 1. Untrain the automatic arousal → floor contraction → ejaculate reflex (years of kegels)
> 2. Widen the arousal window (currently too narrow for any control technique)
> 3. Map the window accurately (levels feel identical right now)
> 4. Control the window actively (stop-start, squeeze)
> 5. Consolidate under realistic conditions (lube, duration, desensitization)

---

## How This Routine Works

| Phase | Job | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **1a — Reflex Retraining** | Break automatic kegel-on-arousal pathway | 2 weeks |
| **1b — Expand the Window** | Widen gap between sensation and point of no return | 3–4 weeks |
| **2 — Learn the Window** | Identify arousal levels accurately, build body awareness | 3–4 weeks |
| **3 — Control the Window** | Stop-start and squeeze with proper timing | 4–6 weeks |
| **4 — Consolidate** | Lube reintroduction, duration building, desensitization | Ongoing |

**Do not skip or merge phases.** Each one builds what the next requires.
Phase 1a exists specifically because of your kegel history — without it, Phases 1b through 3 will plateau without a clear reason why.

## Phase 1a — Kegel Reflex Retraining
### Duration: 2 weeks · 4–5 sessions per week

**The goal:** Insert a conscious pause into the automatic arousal → floor contraction →
ejaculate sequence that years of kegels have deeply grooved. You are not trying to
last longer yet. You are interrupting the reflex at its trigger point.

---

### How to Practice

**No lube. Minimal stimulation. Level 2–3 only.**

- Begin with very slow, light stimulation — significantly less than you normally would
- The **only focus is the pelvic floor** — not arousal level, not duration, not outcome
- The moment you feel the floor begin to tighten or contract — **stop all stimulation immediately**
- Take 3 slow reverse kegel breaths — floor drops completely
- Resume only when floor is fully relaxed again
- Session length: **8 minutes total**
- Ejaculation is not the goal and not a failure — floor awareness is the only metric

**What you are looking for:**
The pelvic floor tightening is the earliest signal in the chain — it fires *before* arousal
climbs noticeably. Your job is to catch it at that earliest point, not after.

> ✅ Think of it as catching the trigger, not the bullet
> ✅ Each time you catch the tightening and release it, you are weakening the automatic reflex
> ✅ Breathe continuously — nose in, mouth out, exhale longer than inhale
> ❌ Do not monitor arousal level during these 2 weeks — floor tension only
> ❌ Do not try to push through the tightening — stop the moment you feel it
> 💡 The floor contraction now fires almost automatically on arousal due to kegel training.
> You are inserting a conscious pause into that sequence before anything else is possible.

**What success looks like:**
- Week 1: Catching the floor tightening sometimes, often missing it and overshooting
- Week 2: Catching it consistently — the pause between stimulation and contraction
  is becoming noticeable and catchable

## Phase 1b — Expand the Window
### Duration: 3–4 weeks · 4–5 sessions per week

**The goal:** Widen the arousal window so there is enough space to eventually apply
control techniques. You are not trying to last longer yet — you are letting the nervous
system experience sustained stimulation without immediately escalating to point of no return.

---

### Arousal Scale

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Minimal, barely noticeable |
| 3–4 | Clearly aroused, comfortable, stable |
| 5–6 | Noticeably building, still controllable |
| 7–8 | Strong urge, approaching point of no return |
| 9 | Point of no return — ejaculation inevitable |
| 10 | Ejaculation |

---

### How to Practice

**No lube. Dry hand. Very slow pace.**

- Begin stimulation and stay at level **3–4** the entire session
- Monitor both arousal level AND floor tension simultaneously now
- If arousal climbs past 5 — stop all stimulation completely
- If floor begins to tighten — stop and reverse kegel before resuming
- Wait until fully back to level 3, floor relaxed, then resume
- Session length: **10 minutes total**, regardless of outcome
- If you reach point of no return — note how far in and aim to catch it slightly earlier next session

> ✅ Breathe continuously — exhale longer than inhale the entire session
> ✅ Keep pelvic floor consciously relaxed throughout — actively release it
> ❌ Do not chase sensation or increase pace — slower is the work here
> ❌ Do not hold breath — single biggest trigger for early ejaculation
> ❌ Do not push to level 6 yet — staying at 3–4 is the entire point of this phase

**What success looks like:**
- Week 1: Reaching 3–5 min at level 3–4 before losing control
- Week 2: Consistently reaching 7–8 min
- Week 3–4: Full 10 min sessions at low arousal feeling stable and manageable

## Phase 2 — Learn the Window
### Duration: 3–4 weeks · 4–5 sessions per week

**The goal:** Map your arousal levels accurately. Right now levels 6, 7, 8, and 9 feel
like the same thing — they happen too fast to distinguish. This phase slows that down
and separates them so you know exactly where you are at any moment.

---

### How to Practice

**Still no lube. Slightly more varied pace.**

- Begin at level 3–4 as before, floor relaxed
- Deliberately and slowly climb to level **6**, hold there for 30 seconds, then bring it back down
- Rest at level 3–4 for 1–2 minutes
- Climb to level **7**, hold for 15 seconds, then back down
- Rest again fully before next climb
- **Never go above 7 intentionally** — 8 is too close to the edge at this stage
- Session length: **12–15 minutes**

**The mapping exercise:**
Each time you descend from a peak, mentally note:
- What did level 6 feel like physically — where in your body specifically?
- What changed between 6 and 7?
- What is the earliest physical signal that arousal is climbing past 5?
- Did the floor start tightening before or after you noticed arousal climbing?

> ✅ Your early warning signal at level 5–6 is your most important discovery in this phase.
> Common signals: floor beginning to tighten, breath wanting to hold naturally,
> inner thigh tension, a specific shift in sensation in the perineum. Find yours.
> ✅ When descending, use reverse kegel breath — actively drop the floor each time
> ❌ Do not rush the descent — take as long as needed to fully return to level 3–4
> ❌ Do not skip rest periods between climbs — arousal stacks if you don't fully reset

**What success looks like:**
- Week 1: Climbing and descending feels clumsy, often overshooting to 8
- Week 2: Consistently catching yourself at 6–7 before escalation
- Week 3–4: Early warning signal identified and reliable — level 5 is clearly
  distinguishable from level 7 every session

## Phase 3 — Control the Window
### Duration: 4–6 weeks · 4–5 sessions per week

**The goal:** Use your now-wider, now-mapped window to actively practice stop-start
and squeeze with proper timing. Duration builds directly in this phase.

---

### Stop-Start — Weeks 1–3 of Phase 3

- Stimulate to level **6**, stop completely
- Wait until fully back to level **3** — do not resume before then
- Resume, climb to 6, stop again
- **Target: 3 complete stop-start cycles per session** before allowing ejaculation
- Session length: 15–20 minutes

> ✅ Stop at level 6 — not 7, not 8. Earlier than feels necessary. That is the point.
> ✅ During the stop, 3 reverse kegel breaths actively bring arousal down faster
> ❌ Do not stop at 8 — that is what failed before. Momentum is already irreversible by then.
> ❌ Do not resume too early — full descent to level 3 every single time

**Progression:** Once 3 cycles feels consistently easy, increase target to 5 cycles.

---

### Squeeze Technique — Weeks 3–6 of Phase 3

Add this as an alternative to stopping completely:

- At level 6, instead of stopping — squeeze the head of the penis firmly for 10–15 seconds
- Combine simultaneously with: slow exhale + reverse kegel
- Resume when back at level 3–4

> ✅ Squeeze + exhale + reverse kegel together is significantly more effective than any one alone
> ✅ This combination is what translates to partnered sex when stopping isn't an option
> 💡 Practice both stop-start and squeeze in the same session once both feel familiar

**What success looks like:**
- Week 2: Consistently completing 3 cycles, total session ~15 min
- Week 4: 5 cycles reliably, arousal window feeling wide and manageable
- Week 6: Sessions reaching 20+ minutes, control beginning to feel natural not effortful

## Phase 4 — Consolidate & Desensitize
### Duration: Ongoing · 3–4 sessions per week

**The goal:** Reintroduce lube, build duration under realistic conditions, and
gradually reduce hypersensitivity to varied stimulation.

---

### Lube Reintroduction — Weeks 1–2

- Repeat Phase 3 stop-start protocol exactly — now with lube
- Expect arousal window to narrow again temporarily — this is normal and expected
- Apply the same level 6 stopping rule strictly
- Within 1–2 weeks the window returns to Phase 3 levels with lube

---

### Duration Building — Weeks 3 Onwards

Once 3 stop-start cycles with lube is reliable, begin building continuous duration:

| Week | Target Continuous Duration |
|---|---|
| Week 3–4 | 2 minutes at level 5–6 |
| Week 5–6 | 4 minutes continuous |
| Week 7–8 | 6–8 minutes continuous |
| Week 9–10 | 10+ minutes with full control |

> ✅ Only increase target when current level feels easy for 3 sessions in a row
> ❌ Never skip a level — each increment trains the nervous system to hold the new threshold

---

### Desensitization — Ongoing Alongside Duration Building

- Once duration targets are being hit, begin varying grip pressure and pace
- Occasionally simulate more intense stimulation — practice maintaining control under it
- This builds tolerance so real-world conditions don't override the training

## How to Know You Are Done With Active Training

Stop active training when all of the following are true for at least 3 weeks consistently:

- [ ] Duration with lube consistently 10+ minutes when desired
- [ ] Point of no return clearly identifiable at level 7 or earlier every session
- [ ] Pelvic floor contraction reflex no longer fires automatically on arousal
- [ ] Stop-start and squeeze feel automatic — not effortful
- [ ] Breath stays relaxed and continuous throughout without conscious effort
- [ ] Pelvic floor stays low-tension throughout — no buildup during stimulation
- [ ] Arousal window feels wide — levels 3 through 8 clearly distinct

**When 6 or more boxes are checked consistently — active training is complete.**

## Maintenance — Indefinite

No specific routine needed once complete. These habits preserve the gains:

- Crocodile breathing 3x per week — keeps baseline pelvic tone low permanently
- Pelvic floor micro-habits from the earlier routine continue indefinitely
- If a stressful period causes regression, return to Phase 3 for 2 weeks —
  it comes back significantly faster the second time

## Common Mistakes That Stall Progress

| Mistake | Why It Fails |
|---|---|
| Skipping Phase 1a | Kegel reflex stays active underneath everything else — progress plateaus silently |
| Stopping at level 8 instead of 6 | Window too narrow, momentum already irreversible |
| Resuming before fully back to level 3 | Arousal stacks — each cycle starts higher than the last |
| Holding breath during stimulation | Single biggest pelvic floor tension trigger |
| Skipping pre-session pelvic reset | Starts hypertonic — window already closed before beginning |
| Skipping Phase 2 | Phase 3 stop-start requires knowing where level 6 is — without mapping it's guesswork |
| Inconsistent practice | Nervous system adaptation requires frequency — 2x per week is not enough |
| Rushing into lube too early | Reintroduce only in Phase 4 — earlier than that collapses the window before it's stable |
