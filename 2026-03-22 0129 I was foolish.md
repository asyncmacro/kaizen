We met and we clicked rapidly, yet I committed a sin.

allowed myself to sink in the sea of love—
And I died there

The warm feeling of mutuality—
Now I'm a shadow in a dark alley.

Jealousy crawling up on my body—
Aching heart, teary eyes but no grave

Writing to tell about my sorrows but I'm alone in cave

Telling my poems to the birds wishing to rely the message—
by the time I finish, they're long gone.

Screaming my poems to reach you—
The fade in the distance.

Who am I to wish for water—
When I'm burning like a fire.

Thinking I was a prince—
To realize I'm just low degree citizen.

You were the sun, I was the moon—
I'm just a moon's reflection in a cup of water.

I'm dying tonight, with no grave.

The night is cold, 
it's gonna rain hard.
What a beautiful night to die on. 
And who knows.
Maybe I did, and you'd never notice.
