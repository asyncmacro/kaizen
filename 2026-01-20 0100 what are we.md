I guess you and I wonder, what are we? And to be honest I don't know , and neither do you.

We live each other but are we just friends, close ones or are we romantic lovers? We don't need titles to define our relationship because I believe it exceeds words, it's far greater than that

If I have to define it, it'd be an eternal flame that I throw myself into even if it costs everything.

Maybe I may not be your soulmate but I believe no lover would love you as much as I do, because you're the flame I hug when life feels cold and if you're cold, I would set the world on fire for you my dear. Let the world burn for the flame of your eyes.

I may be holding just my words, but I am holding inside me a love that no one has ever loved you like before.
