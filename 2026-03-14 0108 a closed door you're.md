Everyone says you're a closed door, unable to be understood
A chaotic girl whose talk is hard to comprehend, yet I can beautifully understand you 
Your silence is a story I feel and empathize with, but I wonder why
Everybody tells me that I am slow to understand but with you I am understanding.
