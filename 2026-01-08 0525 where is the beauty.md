They say everything has its own beauty and I agree, everyone is beautiful on their own way. A snake is beautiful, scary but still can be pretty, the night is beautiful with all it's darkness and silence, even lava is beautiful.

Humans are beautiful also, yet no matter how many person say I'm good looking I still can't believe them and I don't know why. I'm not calling them liars but I just unable to see what they see, what's good in me that you think it's worth mentioning. 

What's the point of being *good looking* when you can't get what you want, the person you love. If it has a meaning then it is being ugly, because since you're not the same league as her, no good genes seen in you.

Then they say you have a good heart but what's the point of having good insides while the outside is rusty.
